Pick your subspecialties and how many questions you want from each, then Start. Questions are drawn at random from the subspecialties you choose and shuffled together. Answers and rationales stay hidden until you press Submit exam, and you can change any answer before then. Your answers can then be saved to the matching section pages (progress bars, scores and misses all update, exactly as if you had answered them there) — you confirm that at the end, and an answer already recorded on a section page is never overwritten. Image-based questions stay on the section pages.
